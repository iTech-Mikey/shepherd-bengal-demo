About us

A proper local dog care business

Shepherd and Bengal Pet Services is driven by a huge passion for animals. Dogs are important to us — and we know they’re important to you.

Amy McAlpine has grown up around animals all her life. Daycare and play take place on our own private field — safe space for the dogs she looks after every day.
