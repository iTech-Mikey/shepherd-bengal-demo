About us

Passionate pet care, done properly

Shepherd & Bengal is a local pet care business built around real love for animals — not a corporate pack-walking service.

Shepherd and Bengal Pet Services is driven by a huge passion for animals. Pets are important to us — and we know they’re important to you. We offer a flexible range of services so your pets get the quality time and attention they need while you get on with life.

Amy McAlpine has grown up around animals all her life. She shares her home with two German Shepherds, four cats, two guinea pigs and a group of marine fish. After years in an office job she built a business around the outdoors, dogs, and proper hands-on care.

All creatures great and small

Dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, birds and more. We look after a wide range of pets and always start with a free meet-and-greet so everyone is comfortable.

What we offer

Simple, reliable pet care

Clear services for busy owners across Hertfordshire.

Dog walking

Group walks (up to 4 dogs) or individual walks, typically 60 minutes. We meet first so we understand your dog’s needs and they feel comfortable with us.

Day visits & day care

Extra care and company during the day when you’re out. Ideal for dogs that need stimulation, a toilet break, or a familiar face while you’re at work.

Pet sitting

Home visits so your pets stay comfortable in their own environment. Great for longer absences or when boarding isn’t the right option.

Puppy visits

Extra support for young dogs — toilet training help, company and gentle stimulation while you’re out.

Cat feeding & small pets

Feeding, litter changes and company for cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, birds and other small pets.

Dog training support

Help with the basics and more. Amy has trained with the Institute of Modern Dog Trainers (IMDT) and keeps things calm and practical.
