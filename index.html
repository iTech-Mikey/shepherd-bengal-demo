About Shepherd & Bengal

Shepherd and Bengal Pet Services is driven by a huge passion for animals. Pets are important to us — and we know they’re important to you. We offer a flexible range of services so your pets get the quality time and attention they need while you get on with life.

Amy McAlpine has grown up around animals all her life. She currently shares her home with two German Shepherds, four cats, two guinea pigs and a group of marine fish. After years in an office-based role she decided to build a business around the things she loves most — the outdoors, dogs, and the freedom to care for animals properly.

Our Services

Simple, reliable care for dogs and other pets across Hertfordshire.

Dog Walking

Group walks (up to 4 dogs) or individual walks, typically 60 minutes. Perfect when work, health or other commitments keep you from getting out. We chat first so we understand your dog’s needs and make sure they’re comfortable with us.

Day Visits & Day Care

Extra care and attention during the day when you’re out. Ideal for dogs that need company, stimulation or a toilet break while you’re at work.

Pet Sitting

Home visits so your pets stay comfortable in their own environment. Great for longer absences or when boarding isn’t the right option.

Puppy Visits

Extra support for young dogs — toilet training help, company and gentle stimulation while you’re out.

Cat Feeding & Small Pets

Feeding, litter changes and company for cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, birds and other small pets. We look after all creatures great and small.

Dog Training

Support with the basics and more. Amy has trained with the Institute of Modern Dog Trainers (IMDT) and brings a calm, practical approach.
