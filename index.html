About us

Passionate local pet care

A family business built around real love for animals — not a corporate pack-walking service.

Shepherd and Bengal Pet Services is driven by a huge passion for animals. Pets are important to us — and we know they’re important to you. We offer a flexible range of services so your pets get the quality time and attention they need while you get on with life.

Amy McAlpine has grown up around animals all her life. She shares her home with two German Shepherds, four cats, two guinea pigs and a group of marine fish. After years in an office role she built a business around the outdoors, dogs, and proper hands-on care.

Our services

Pet care for every type of animal

Simple, reliable care for dogs and other pets across Hertfordshire.

Dog walking

Group walks (up to 4 dogs) or individual walks, typically 60 minutes. We meet first so we understand your dog’s needs.

Day visits & day care

Extra care and company during the day when you’re out. Ideal for dogs that need stimulation or a toilet break.

Pet sitting

Home visits so your pets stay comfortable in their own environment. Great when boarding isn’t the right option.

Puppy visits

Extra support for young dogs — toilet training help, company and gentle stimulation while you’re out.

Cat feeding & small pets

Feeding, litter changes and company for cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, birds and other small pets.

Dog training support

Help with the basics and more. Amy has trained with the Institute of Modern Dog Trainers (IMDT).
